## About

**Legis Lectiones** is a personal study environment designed for Law School students. It transforms passive reading into active recall through a dynamic, interactive platform. The project focuses on reinforcing legal knowledge using varied question formats and instant feedback.

### Key Features

- **Multiple-choice questions** with plausible distractors
- **Drag-and-drop exercises** for concept association
- **Completion tasks** to test recall
- **Instant sound feedback** for correct and incorrect answers
- **Progress tracking** including views, likes, and completion rates

---

## Philosophy

> *"Repetition is the mother of retention."*

The methodology behind **Legis Lectiones** is simple:

1.  **Active Recall:** Retrieve information instead of just re-reading.
2.  **Varied Formats:** Encounter the same concept through different exercises to build flexible knowledge.
3.  **Immediate Feedback:** Correct mistakes in the moment to strengthen neural pathways.
